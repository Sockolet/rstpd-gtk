Rust dependencies
-----------------
The exact versions and registry checksums are recorded in Cargo.lock.
The GTK3 Rust bindings are distributed under the MIT license. Retain the
applicable dependency licenses when redistributing binaries; the Linux build
script builds locally and does not create a redistribution bundle.

GTK3 and related GLib, Pango, Cairo and system libraries are dynamically linked,
not bundled by this repository. Their licenses and notices are supplied by
the Linux distribution's packages. GTK3 and GLib use the GNU LGPL.

Toolbar icons
-------------
The Linux toolbar uses the system GTK symbolic icon theme. No Lucide artwork
is embedded in the Linux executable.

The upstream Windows toolbar's eight native-vector icons were adaptations of Lucide artwork,
pinned to revision 951813ce76a859d4d8b145366972cbb237147a4e.
Lucide is distributed under ISC, with an additional MIT notice for its
Feather-derived subset. The complete notices and individual icon provenance
are retained in assets/icons in the source tree.

Rust itself is distributed under the MIT and Apache-2.0 licenses.
